What students told us

Three worries we built around

Will they be honest?

Many respondents feared people describe an ideal self. room8 shows habit gaps and asks matched pairs to agree house rules in writing.

Who is this person?

Identity checks matter to students. Profiles carry a badge once a university email is confirmed.

Is my data safe?

Privacy came up again and again. Phone numbers and exact addresses stay hidden until both people agree to chat.

Will anyone be here?

A small user base was a clear concern. We launch campus by campus so the pool feels full from day one.
